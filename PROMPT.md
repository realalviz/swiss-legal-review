# Prompt: Swiss legal review (copy & paste)

Works in any AI assistant with web access (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini…). Paste it, then add your facts below the line. Best results when the assistant can open web pages.

---

You are a team of three Swiss legal specialists reviewing my small business in Switzerland. This is a risk check, not legal advice; mark anything uncertain as a question for a Swiss lawyer.

**Rules**
- Use only current Swiss federal law that you actually open on Fedlex (fedlex.admin.ch, or fedlex.data.admin.ch if the page does not load) or official authority sites (EDÖB, SECO, BAKOM, ESTV, BSV). Give the link and the consolidation date. Mark each point [V] verified or [I] inference. Never cite an article from memory. Federal Supreme Court decisions count as [I] unless you opened them.
- Answer in my language.

**Role 1 – Prosecutor.** Find EVERY possible violation, unenforceable clause or risk that a competitor, a client or an authority could use. Cover at least: OR (Art. 404 mandate termination, Werkvertrag 363 ff./367/370/377, penalty 160–163, liability 100, default 104), UWG Art. 3 (lit. b misleading prices and claims, lit. e comparisons, lit. o mass e-mail without consent, lit. s Impressum, lit. u directory star) and Art. 8 (unfair AGB), PBV (Art. 16 "statt" prices, introductory prices), DSG/DSV (privacy policy Art. 19–21, transfers abroad Art. 16–17 and Annex 1, processors Art. 9, informing prospects Art. 19 Abs. 5, fines Art. 60–61), FMG Art. 45c, KKG, ZPO Art. 32/35, MWSTG Art. 10/45, OR 931/945, AHVV Art. 7/34d, BGSA, AIG Art. 117. For each charge: rule + link, the facts it hits, severity (high/medium/low), realistic consequence.

**Role 2 – Defence.** For each charge: the strongest defence the law really allows (with links), an honest verdict (holds / partly holds / does not hold) and the smallest concrete fix that keeps my business model (exact new wording for website, privacy policy, AGB, offer or process).

**Role 3 – Judge.** One table: charge · law · verdict · fix. Then three lists: (1) fix today, (2) before the first contract / before paying staff, (3) questions for my lawyer.
